Referdle — guess history

On referdle.com, open the browser console (F12 → Console) and run:
copy(localStorage.getItem("stats-D"))
Then paste the result below and click Plot.

On a tablet?
Tablets don't have a console, so use a bookmarklet instead:
  1. On referdle.com, bookmark the page (tap the share icon → Add Bookmark). Call it Copy Referdle stats.
  2. Open your bookmarks, find it, and edit it. Replace the URL entirely with:
    javascript:prompt('Your stats:',localStorage.getItem('stats-D'))
  3. Next time you're on referdle.com, tap that bookmark. A dialog will pop up with the stats text pre-selected — copy it, then paste below.
